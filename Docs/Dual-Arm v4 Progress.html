What the probes found

The fingers could barely grip

The fingers kept Newton's default contact settings, which are soft and scale with the brick's mass. Real grip saturated at 1–2 N whatever was commanded, and the pads sank through the brick wall. Fixed in plan r4 with stiffer finger contacts.

grip (7.1 N asked)1.2–2.0 N → 6.9 Nfinger sink0.9–1.6 mm → 0.10–0.24 mmcarry slip1.60 mm → 0.095 mm

The weld check passed unseated bricks

At 2× a brick resting on the stud tops passed the old check, and the probe counted passes while the brick was still moving. r4 tests once, after release, with the brick at rest, and tightens the height band.

height band−0.3s…+1.0s mm → −0.5s…+0.3s mmwhen testedany frame → at rest, after release

Newton was silently dropping contacts

Past 1 million triangle pairs Newton skips the rest, with only a printed warning. Every cube run hit it. The buffer is now 4 million, and the probes abort if any buffer overflows.

peak pairs (cube)1,163,533warnings1274 → 0

The simulation slows down as the build grows

Every seated brick keeps thousands of contacts with its neighbours, and MuJoCo's solver pays for all of them. Once bricks are welded those contacts carry no information, so putting the finished structure in one collision group drops them.

S3 built, 16 substeps0.12× real time → 0.68–0.81×contacts20,800 → 2,400

At 1× the finger pad is wider than the brick

The FR3 fingertip is 17.5 mm wide and a 2x4's short side is 16 mm. When a brick goes in flush beside another, the pad overhang brushes the neighbour in every cube run. At 2× the brick side is 32 mm, so there is no overhang.

Decisions coming to you

Plan revision r5: reviewed once, changes requested, paused hereThe direction holds: welded bricks in one collision group, real time measured at the start and end of a build, the declared 1× finger graze as its own event class, and P2's stricter wrist-camera check. The review asks for protocol fixes before it comes to you:
· At 8 substeps MuJoCo clamps the welds' time constant from 2 ms to 4.2 ms, so welds get about 4× softer, and grouping makes them the only thing holding the structure. Weld deflection has to be measured.
· If a P1 item fails at 8 substeps, it gets re-run at 16 before it counts.
· The real-time test runs at both brick scales.
· Penetration between seated bricks is spelled out for grouped and ungrouped runs.
· The finger graze gets a force bound.
· Two P1 definition changes go on your approval list.

Watch it run

The simulator is the prototype at HEAD with the r4 finger fix and the buffer fix. Collision-free motion, the vision pipeline and the new weld check aren't wired in yet, so expect the P0′ behaviour in the table above. It also slows down as bricks accumulate; the collision-group fix isn't in the cell yet.
